# Sell Plan — Apex Capital Admin Services

## Goal
Exit as a **working micro-SaaS asset** (code + brand + ops), or wait for first revenue and re-price as a cash-flow business.

## Recommended ask (as of pack date)
| Scenario | Ask | Walk-away |
|----------|-----|-----------|
| As-is (~$0 live revenue) | **$22,000 – $28,000** | **$12,000** |
| After 30 days + documented traffic + ≥1 live charge | **$35,000 – $50,000** | **$25,000** |
| Steady ≥$1k/mo profit | **24–36× monthly profit** | Negotiate |

## Where to sell (in order)
1. **Acquire.com** — primary. Serious SaaS buyers, escrow.
2. **Flippa** — secondary if Acquire is slow; expect more haggling.
3. **Warm intros** — Indie Hackers, X/Twitter founders, operators who want a draft engine.
4. Empire Flippers / larger brokers — only after real monthly profit.

## 14-day sell process
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Finalize ask. Create Acquire account. Read LISTING_DRAFT.md. |
| 2 | Screenshot pack: homepage, Modes, /go/notice intake, Stripe test/live note, IG. |
| 3 | Publish listing. Price at top of ask band. |
| 4–10 | Answer buyer questions same-day. Share ASSET_INVENTORY (not secrets). |
| 11–12 | LOI → escrow. Do **not** transfer domain/Stripe until funds clear. |
| 13–14 | Run BUYER_TRANSFER_PACK.md checklist. 7-day support window starts. |

## What not to do
- Don’t invent revenue or traffic.
- Don’t transfer DNS/Stripe before escrow release.
- Don’t include customer PII dumps in the listing.
- Don’t remove engines or Modes to “simplify” — buyer paid for the catalog.

## Pre-list boost (optional, raises price)
1. One real `cs_live_` charge ($24).
2. GA4 screenshot: 14-day sessions + top landings `/go/*`.
3. IG profile 100% + pinned Notice Reel.
4. Confirm Netlify + Neon + Stripe access are transferable.

## Fees (typical)
- Acquire / Flippa: success fee % of sale (check current site terms).
- Escrow: included or small fee — use platform escrow only.
