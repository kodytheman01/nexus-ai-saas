Post **2–4×/day** across IG (primary), then same day cross-post FB + TikTok + YT Shorts when energy allows. One Mode theme per day keeps the brand clear.

## Week 2 — Pain × Mode
Rotate hook bank variants. Same walkthrough UI, new VO/text overlay if needed.
- Notice: late rent, vacate, deposit
- Grant: FOA, budget narrative, compliance
- Bid: change order, scope
- Offer: rejection letter, promotion

## Week 3 — Authority without lying

## Week 4 — Amplify winners
- Remake top 3 hooks
- Duet/stitch 5 pain posts
- Collab DMs (10 landlords, 10 grant writers)
- Publish 1 longer YT (3–5 min) Mode tour if Shorts traction
- Prep paid: screenshot organic winners for Meta Ads library

3. Cross-post to FB (and TT if time)
