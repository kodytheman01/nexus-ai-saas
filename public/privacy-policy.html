Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Apex Capital Admin Services ("Apex," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use https://apexcapitaladmin.com and related services.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address at checkout (to deliver your order and support follow-up).
  • Written input you submit to an engine (to generate your deliverable).
  • Basic site usage data via Google Analytics (pages visited, device/browser, approximate location) in aggregate.
  • Optional support-chat messages sent to Apex Concierge.

We do not collect or store your payment card number, expiration date, or CVC. Payment details are handled directly by Stripe under Stripe's privacy practices.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate and deliver purchased deliverables.
  • To send transactional emails (delivery, receipts, support).
  • To maintain order records for support, accounting, and fraud prevention.
  • To understand aggregate traffic and improve the platform.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third parties

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • OpenAI — processes intake text to generate drafts.
  • Google (Gmail / Workspace) — transactional email.
  • Google Analytics — aggregate traffic measurement.
  • Meta Pixel / Conversions API — advertising measurement when configured.

4. Data retention

Order records are kept as long as reasonably necessary for support, accounting, and legal obligations. Engine inputs are retained only as needed to deliver output and provide limited support, then may be deleted or anonymized.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Contact admin@apexcapitaladmin.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

6. User data deletion

To request deletion of your data associated with our Meta/Instagram app or website, email admin@apexcapitaladmin.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your Facebook/Instagram username or the email used at checkout. We will delete or anonymize associated personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Children's privacy

This service is for adults and professional use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
